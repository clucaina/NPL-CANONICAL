## What changed in C12

C12 converts the prior specialist engines into a coordinated product. It introduces a Cognitive Fabric with 15 specialized subsystems, a Capability Graph, governed Signal Bus, selective routing, bounded current Workspace, salience/attention routing, feedback-loop controls, canonical entity resolution, global focus and universal WHY / WHAT NEXT / knowledge/action routing.

The design principle is brain-like coordination at a software-systems level: distributed specialized systems, selective communication, recurrence, memory, contextual modulation and consolidation. It does **not** claim biological brain simulation.
